HOW IT WORKS

From script to narration in three steps

Sonora turns your written script into an AI voice narration while keeping the generation and export controls in the studio.

  1. Write and shape. Paste or write a script, then add optional emotion and delivery tags to guide the reading.
  2. Choose and preview. Select an engine and voice, generate the narration, and listen to the completed audio.
  3. Export. Download MP3 as a guest, or sign in with Google for WAV, FLAC, AAC, and OGG exports.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How does the daily word allowance work?

Guests can submit up to 50 whitespace-separated words per IP per UTC day. Signed-in Google accounts can submit up to 1,000 words per account per UTC day. Words in the script and delivery tags both count. Allowances reset at 00:00 UTC.

Which audio formats can I export?

Guests can download MP3. WAV, FLAC, AAC, and OGG downloads require Google sign-in.

Will tags produce an exact voice delivery?

Tags guide delivery, but generated output is not guaranteed to match the requested delivery exactly.

Can I generate, preview, or export audio without JavaScript?

JavaScript is required for generation, preview, and export.
